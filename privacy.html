Privacy Policy

Toned

Last updated 9 August 2026

Toned does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no tracking. The app makes no network calls at all.

What stays on your device

Every stage of the chain runs locally on your iPhone. The photographs you open, the negatives and prints you make, the frames you keep and your settings are all stored only on your device.

Photo library

Toned asks for permission to add photos to your library only so it can save a print, negative, film strip, contact sheet or clip that you chose to export. It has add-only access: it does not read your photo library. You pick each photograph yourself through the system photo picker.

Purchases

The optional one-time Toned Plus unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Toned never sees your payment information. The purchase is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored from Settings inside the app.

Data collection

None. Toned contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising, no attribution and no crash reporting. This matches the "Data Not Collected" label on its App Store page.

Children

Toned is rated 4+, is suitable for all ages, and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
