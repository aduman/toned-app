Toned Motion Privacy Policy

Toned Motion processes videos and photographs on your device. Imported source videos, project edits, portable recipes, preview images and finished results are stored in the app's local container. Toned Motion does not upload this media to an application server. You choose when and where to save a result or share a file.

Permissions and purchases

Photos permission is requested only to add a finished video or image. Video import uses system file and photo pickers. Purchases use Apple's StoreKit and App Store services. Apple processes payments and provides verified purchase status. Toned Motion does not receive payment-card details.

Accounts and local information

No Toned Motion account is required. The app integrates no analytics or advertising service. Device storage information is used to check space for export, file information to manage local projects, and local preferences to remember appearance choices.

Deleting your data

You can delete projects and finished results in the app. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to your device's backup and restore behavior. Copies explicitly exported to Photos, Files or another service are managed by those destinations and are not deleted when you remove a local project.
