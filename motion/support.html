Toned Motion Support

For help, email membrain.app@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version and what you were doing. You do not need to send personal videos.

Start editing

Choose the included example or import a video. Pick a recipe, refine it with Adjust, choose a motion mode, and use Clip to trim or frame your result.

Save your result

Choose Export, review the settings, and choose Make video. Save the completed result to Photos or Files, or share it. If you cancel the destination, the completed render stays in your project so you can save it later.

Restore Plus

Open Settings and choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that purchased Toned Motion Plus. Purchases in the companion Toned app are separate. If the App Store is unavailable, free editing and up to 1080p export remain available.

Manage local storage

Manage projects from Projects. Settings also lets you remove local export results while retaining source videos and edits. Copies you saved to Photos, Files or other apps are managed separately.
