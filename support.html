Frequently asked

How do I make a print?

Choose a photograph, then work along the benches in order: Film, Develop, Print, Finish. Load a stock and set the exposure, push or pull the development, set the enlarger exposure and the paper grade, then dodge, burn and tone. Hold the compare button at any point to see the photograph you started with.

Where is the negative?

On the Develop bench. It is a real intermediate, with the orange mask on colour stocks and a clear base on black and white, and you can export it on its own.

What can I do without paying?

Every stage of the chain, at full resolution, on your own photograph. Three film stocks, two papers, a toner and three film formats are free, and you can use and look at all the rest too. A frame built from free content exports as a print, negative, film strip, contact sheet or clip, up to 8x10 at 300 dpi, carrying a small TONED mark in the film's own edge lettering.

What does Toned Plus add?

All thirteen stocks, all five papers, all four toners, every film format and print size, split-grade printing, export at full size with no mark, and as many kept frames as you like. It is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription and there will not be one.

I bought Plus on another device.

Open Settings inside Toned and tap Restore Purchases. Plus is tied to your Apple ID.

Do my photos leave my device?

No. Toned makes no network calls at all. See the privacy policy.

Why does my print look different from another app's filter?

Because it is not a filter. The tones come out of a characteristic curve per dye layer, a real inversion to a negative, and a second inversion through the paper. Grain sits in the developed density rather than on top of the file, and halation lands around a blown highlight rather than glowing over it.

Does it open RAW files?

Yes. RAW files are decoded with their highlights above white intact, which is the detail the film curve needs. Toned edits photographs from your library; it is not a camera.
